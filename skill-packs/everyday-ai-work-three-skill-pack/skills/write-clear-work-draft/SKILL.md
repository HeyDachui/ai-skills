# 工作文章 / Clear Work Writing

把选题、材料或口述内容写成一篇结构完整、清楚、方便继续修改的工作稿。目标不是一次写成名作，而是先得到一版能看、能改、能继续使用的文字。

Turn a topic, source material, or spoken notes into a complete, clear, editable work draft. The goal is a usable first draft, not a claim of perfect writing.

## 怎么做 / What to do

1. 从上下文判断读者、用途、渠道和大致长度；只有缺少的信息会改变文章方向时才问一个问题。  
   Infer the reader, purpose, channel, and approximate length; ask one question only when the answer would change the direction.
2. 区分使用者原话、已有事实、外部材料、AI推断和未知。不要把AI补充冒充使用者经历或确定事实。  
   Separate the user's words, known facts, outside material, AI inference, and unknowns. Do not present AI additions as personal experience or confirmed fact.
3. 先确定文章要解决的问题和核心判断，再选择适合用途的结构。不要把所有内容强制写成同一个模板。  
   Identify the problem and central judgment, then choose a structure that fits the purpose. Do not force every draft into one formula.
4. 写完整初稿：开头让读者知道为什么要看，正文按清楚关系展开，结尾给出结论、行动或自然收束。  
   Write a complete draft: establish relevance early, develop the body with clear relationships, and end with a conclusion, action, or natural close.
5. 使用普通、具体、可信的语言。删除空话和重复，但保留必要情绪、专业名称、数字、条件和例外。  
   Use plain, specific, credible language. Remove filler and repetition while preserving necessary emotion, names, numbers, conditions, and exceptions.
6. 检查逻辑跳跃、事实补造、绝对承诺、对象身份和前后矛盾。不能确认的内容继续标明未知。  
   Check for logical gaps, invented facts, absolute promises, identity errors, and contradictions. Keep unverified claims visible as unknown.

## 默认输出 / Default output

先交付可直接阅读的完整稿件。只有以下内容会帮助继续修改时，再在文后简短列出：

- 需要使用者确认的1—3项；
- 没有使用或没有核实的材料。

Deliver the complete readable draft first. Add up to three confirmation items or unused/unverified sources only when they help the next revision.

## 完成标准 / Done when

- 交付的是一篇从开头到结尾都能直接阅读和继续修改的完整稿，不是提纲、模板或写作建议；
- 读者能看清这篇稿要解决什么、主要判断是什么、各部分为什么按当前顺序出现；
- 名称、日期、数字、决定、条件和不确定性与材料一致，缺失事实没有被AI补成确定结论；
- 删除空话后仍保留必要情绪、语境和专业名称，没有把所有稿件磨成同一种AI语气。

The result is a complete draft rather than an outline, the reader can follow its purpose and reasoning, facts and uncertainty remain faithful to the source, and necessary human context is not flattened into generic AI prose.

## 退出边界 / Exit boundary

如果已有完整稿件只需要改得更易读，使用旧产品中的`make-it-readable`。法律、学术、医疗、正式品牌文案或其他高风险专业写作应转到专业能力并接受相应核验。

If a complete draft only needs readability editing, use `make-it-readable` from the separate beginner safety-net product. Route legal, academic, medical, formal brand, and other high-stakes writing to specialist capabilities and review.
